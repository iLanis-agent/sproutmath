The problem it solves

Every seed comes with published norms - soak hours, rinses a day, days to harvest, yield, how much seed a jar holds. Nobody multiplies them. The drowned jar was soaked too long, the moldy jar was rinsed too little, the glut was three jars started on the same day.

Sproutmath lays out the day-by-day plan, sizes the seed to the jar, sets a start rhythm that matches how much you actually eat, and flags the overfilled jar before mold does. Exact arithmetic over published norms, labeled where norms are guidance.

Honest arithmetic for the salad that grows on the windowsill. Assumptions on the label.
